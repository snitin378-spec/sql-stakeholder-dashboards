# Leadership Snapshot Dashboard

## User
Founder / CEO

## Purpose
Provides leadership with a quick view of overall business health, including revenue, paid orders, AOV, conversion rate, refund rate, and week-over-week movement.

## Live Dashboard
[Open Leadership Snapshot in Metabase]( https://metabase.topfolio.in/dashboard/56-leadership-snapshot-dashboard?acquisition_channel=&country=&date_range=2026-05-16~2026-06-14&device_type=&payment_method=)

## Key KPIs
- Revenue
- Paid Orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Conversion Rate
- Refund Rate

## Key Visuals
- Daily Revenue — Prior Week Comparison
- Daily Paid Orders
- Revenue by Country
- Revenue by Acquisition Channel
- Daily Business Performance diagnostic table

## Filters
- Date Range
- Country
- Acquisition Channel
- Payment Method
- Device Type
